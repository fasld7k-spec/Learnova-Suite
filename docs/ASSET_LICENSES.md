# Asset Licenses And Credits

This file is included to make the CodeCanyon review and buyer handoff clear.

## Included With The Download

- Application source code, database schemas, seed scripts, tests, and documentation are included.
- Included course covers, icons, SVG illustrations, and sample PDFs in `public/` are editable bundled preview assets for this product package.
- Open-source npm dependencies are installed by the buyer with `npm install`; they are not bundled in the item ZIP.

## Not Included

- No premium third-party templates, marketplace items, paid media packs, stock videos, or paid icon packs are included.
- No payment gateway account, SMTP account, video streaming account, AI provider key, S3/R2 bucket, analytics account, or live domain is included.
- Demo screenshots created during development are excluded from the CodeCanyon package.

## Buyer Responsibilities

- Replace demo media with their own licensed course covers, videos, PDFs, and brand assets before production launch.
- Verify that any added photos, videos, fonts, and marketing assets are licensed for commercial use.
- Keep protected lesson videos and downloadable resources in private storage such as S3/R2/Bunny/Cloudflare Stream, not in public folders.

## Fonts And Icons

- The UI uses a system sans-serif stack by default.
- Icons are rendered through `lucide-react`, installed from npm under its open-source license.
- Direct open-source dependency notes are listed in `docs/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

## Preview And Demo Notes

The packaged `preview.png`, `thumbnail.png`, and screenshot archive are generated from the included Learnova Suite demo UI. They do not rely on a paid third-party template, premium stock pack, or external marketplace asset.

If you use different assets in a hosted live preview or CodeCanyon item screenshots, list those preview-only assets in the item description and state whether they are included in the download.
